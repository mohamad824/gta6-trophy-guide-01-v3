      Roadmap S1–S7

        Save — export & import

        Progress saves automatically in this browser (localStorage). Export a file to back it up or move it; import a file or pasted JSON to restore it.

        Roadmap to the platinum — seven stages, in play order

        Story first, then side strands, activities, collectibles, robberies, and the 100% cleanup. The platinum unlocks automatically once the other 54 are earned. Budget around 200 hours; some guides estimate 250–300 hours.

        Missables & Watch — what can actually go wrong

        Five guides count 1 possibly missable trophy; some lists add a second; Push Square counts none. This page shows all three readings and the safe play for each.

        Save strategy (so a miss never costs a playthrough)

        1. 15 manual saves + 1 autosave. Save at the start and end of every chapter; rotate 5 spares for free-roam cleanup. [1]
        2. Optional missions that vanish (black icon, pink letter) do not affect any trophy. [1]
        3. Quest givers who vanish for a while (Kenton) come back. Some fences close permanently — sell stolen goods before their related story mission, with no trophy impact. [1]
        4. Collectible magazines in story buildings that lock permanently also spawn elsewhere. [1]
        5. Hourglass quests fail after a short window and are, in PowerPyx's words, “not relevant for trophies” — except Yago's race, which can lock Yago's chain. [1][17]
        6. Criminal Profile only changes police behaviour; a low profile means police are more likely to shoot on sight. Relationship status between Jason and Lucia does not affect any trophy. [1]
